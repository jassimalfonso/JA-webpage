Pick a place. Make a page about it.

The brief: a single page about your hometown. I picked Dubai — the city that became home.

A header, an intro, four cards (Opera, Mall, Desert Safari, Miracle Garden) and a short profile at the bottom.

How it works

[03]

  1. Cards in a row

    Each place is a card: image, heading, a paragraph. Flexbox lines them up and wraps them on smaller screens.

    .card-container { display: flex; flex-wrap: wrap; gap: 1rem; }

  2. Two typefaces

    A serif for the headline, a clean sans for reading — the first time I chose fonts on purpose.
