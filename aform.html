Challenge

[01]

Every episode, a studio different room.

The show goes to the guest: their office, their majlis, their site. Great for the conversation — hard on the audio. One week it's an echoey meeting room, the next it's an AC unit humming behind the mics, the next it's wind and traffic.

The listener shouldn't notice any of that. My job is to keep the sound consistent from episode to episode — same loudness, same clarity, same show — whatever room it was recorded in.

How it works

[05]

  1. Listen to the room first

    Every location has its own problem — echo, hum, wind. Each gets fixed for what it is, not with one preset for everything.

  2. Clean, but keep the voice

    Noise down, room tamed, fillers trimmed by ear. Push any of it too far and the hosts stop sounding like themselves.

  3. Level to one target

    Every episode is brought to the same loudness, so nobody reaches for the volume between episodes — or between guests.

    target ≈ −16 LUFS · peaks below −1 dB

  4. Then the reels

    Three to six 20–30 second clips per episode, captioned three words at a time in the show's look — most people watch with the sound off.

Learned

[06]

The best edit is the one nobody hears.

  • Consistency is the brand listeners forgive a room; they don't forgive a jump in volume
  • Tools draft, ears decide auto noise removal and filler cuts get checked, every time
  • Process beats heroics bi-weekly, on location, around everyone's day jobs
